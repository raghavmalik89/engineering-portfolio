# Brand Guide

## Positioning
Raghav Malik is positioned as:

- Systems Architect
- Product Engineer
- Industrial IoT / Embedded / RF Systems specialist
- Technical Lead
- Engineering consulting-style problem solver

## Core Message

> I design engineering systems that survive the real world.

## Supporting Message

Systems architecture, embedded electronics, RF communications, industrial IoT, product engineering, and field-deployed systems across mining, aerospace, robotics, and medical accelerator environments.

## Tone
- Confident
- Grounded
- Technical
- Clear
- Mature
- Evidence-based

## Avoid
- Hype
- Generic resume language
- Tech-bro phrasing
- Overly flashy design
- Inflated claims

## Visual Style
- Dark theme
- Industrial premium
- Clean typography
- Strong whitespace
- Real engineering photos
- Technical diagrams
- Subtle accents

## Suggested Color Direction
- Deep navy / charcoal background
- Steel grey surfaces
- Industrial orange accent
- Muted mining green secondary accent
- White/off-white body text

## Typography Direction
- Inter
- Geist
- IBM Plex Sans

## Visual Assets to Prioritize
- PCB photos
- Field deployment photos
- System diagrams
- Test setup images
- RF analyzer screenshots
- Product/demo photos
