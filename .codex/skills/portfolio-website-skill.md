# Portfolio Website Skill

## Purpose
This skill defines how to build and maintain Raghav Malik's engineering portfolio and consulting-style website.

The site should present Raghav as a systems architect and product engineer who can design, build, validate, and deploy real-world engineering systems.

## Core Principle
This is not a decorative resume site. It is a technical credibility platform.

Every page should help one of these outcomes:

- Get an interview.
- Support an interview discussion.
- Demonstrate engineering judgment.
- Provide reusable proof of capability.
- Create a consulting-style professional presence.

## Website MVP
The MVP should include:

- Home
- About
- Selected Systems / Case Studies
- Engineering Decisions
- Engineering Notebook
- Resume / CV Download
- Contact

## Case Study Priority
Flagship case studies:

1. CC1310 custom industrial IoT sensor
2. SmartRoller / mining sensor system
3. GET Vision / AI mining camera system
4. Proton therapy commissioning
5. Space propulsion electronics
6. Robotics / WAAM automation

## Home Page Goals
The Home page must communicate:

- Who Raghav is.
- What engineering domain he works in.
- What kinds of systems he builds.
- Why his experience is different.
- Where to go next.

Suggested hero message:

> I design engineering systems that survive the real world.

Support message:

> Systems architecture, embedded electronics, RF communications, industrial IoT, and field-deployed product engineering across mining, aerospace, robotics, and medical accelerator systems.

## About Page Goals
The About page should explain the career pattern, not just list jobs.

Themes:

- Systems thinking
- Product architecture
- Field reality
- Business judgment
- Manufacturing awareness
- Technical leadership

## Selected Systems Goals
This is the main proof section. It should show projects as systems, not job entries.

Each project should answer:

- What was the problem?
- Why was it hard?
- What constraints mattered?
- What architecture was chosen?
- What trade-offs were made?
- What was tested?
- What survived real deployment?
- What did Raghav contribute?

## Engineering Decisions Goals
This page should show how Raghav thinks.

Good article ideas:

- BLE vs Sub-GHz for industrial sensing
- Why demo firmware is not product firmware
- Designing battery-powered sensors for harsh environments
- Why field deployment changes product architecture
- Sensor payload design for low-power RF systems
- What mining products teach about reliability

## Engineering Notebook Goals
This page keeps the site alive.

It can include:

- Short technical notes
- Build logs
- Test observations
- RF experiments
- PCB notes
- Firmware learnings
- Product architecture reflections

## Visual Style
Use:

- Dark theme
- Clean typography
- Strong spacing
- Industrial colors
- Real engineering photos
- Simple diagrams
- Minimal animation

Avoid:

- Template portfolio look
- Overly flashy animations
- Unreadable text
- Fake 3D graphics
- Generic icons as a substitute for real content

## Definition of Done
A page is done when:

- The purpose is clear.
- The copy is specific and credible.
- The layout is responsive.
- The page has a clear next action.
- It supports the overall positioning.
- It avoids confidential details.
- It can be reused as a career asset.
